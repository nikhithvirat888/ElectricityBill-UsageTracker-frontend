Questions

Is my data private?

Yes. You log in with your own account, and passwords are stored encrypted. You only see your own readings, bills and appliances.

How is the estimated bill calculated?

Your units are split across tariff slabs, each with its own rate, and a fixed charge is added. The slabs are configurable to match your electricity board.

Can I edit or delete a bill?

Yes. Open Bill History, then use View, Edit or Delete on any bill.

What if I enter a wrong meter reading?

The app checks that readings are positive and that the current reading is not lower than the previous one, and shows a clear message if not.
